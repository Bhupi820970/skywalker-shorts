Privacy Policy

Skywalker Uploader is used only by its owner to upload videos to the owner's own YouTube channel. It uses YouTube API Services and Google OAuth scopes youtube.upload and youtube.readonly. It does not collect, store, sell or share any personal data of other users. OAuth tokens are stored only as encrypted secrets for the owner's automation. Use of YouTube is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and Google Privacy Policy. Access can be revoked anytime at Google security settings.
